AHD Wellbeing365 · Delivery method

How the daily nudges reach people

The pilot sends four bilingual wellbeing nudges a day. Who receives them, and how reliably, depends on the delivery method. This compares a personal 1:1 message against a shared Teams channel.

✓

Recommendation — deliver 1:1, add a channel only as a complement

1:1 guarantees every person receives every nudge and keeps it personal. A branded channel can run alongside as a shared feed, but should not replace 1:1 — members can quietly leave a channel and fall out of reach.

Recommended

1:1 personal nudge

A direct message to each employee

  • Reaches 100% of the group — a bot chat cannot be "left"
  • Muting silences the ping, but the nudge still arrives
  • Private and personal — feels addressed to me
  • $0 — standard Microsoft 365 connectors
  • Sender label reads "Workflows"; the card itself is branded AHD Thrive365
Trade-offs

Teams channel

One post to a shared space

  • Members can leave, hide or mute the channel — reach erodes
  • Broadcast feel — less personal for wellbeing
  • Four posts a day in one place invites mute fatigue
  • Branded sender needs a paid plan on a retiring connector
  • Reactions and activity are visible to everyone in the channel
